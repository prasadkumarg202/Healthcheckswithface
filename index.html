📋 Camera & Lighting Tips

  • External webcam (e.g. Logitech C920) gives dramatically better results than a built-in laptop camera — higher resolution, better low-light sensor.
  • Sit facing a lamp — even, frontal light is critical. Avoid windows behind you.
  • The Quality indicator above shows the live camera assessment. Aim for 🟢 Good.
  • If signal is poor after 30 s, the app automatically extends to 60 s.
  • Use the camera dropdown to switch between laptop and external webcam without reloading.

⚠️ Proof-of-concept only. Not a certified medical device. SpO₂ requires IR illumination for clinical accuracy. Do not use for medical decisions.
